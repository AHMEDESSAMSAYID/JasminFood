Sourcing services

What we do on your behalf inside Türkiye

Supplier search

We look for the right plant or farm for your item in its own producing region, including those that never appear online.

Supplier evaluation

We compare the shortlist on price, specification, capacity and discipline, and rule out anyone whose goods will not clear your market.

Commercial negotiation

We negotiate price, payment terms and production time in Turkish, and fix what was agreed in a written purchase order.

Quality control

Samples, laboratory analyses and a site inspection of the actual goods before loading, with a photographed report.

Export documentation

Preparing and checking the health certificates, certificate of origin, packing list and bill of lading before departure.

Logistics coordination

Booking with the carrier and following the loading, the container and its temperature through to the port of arrival.
