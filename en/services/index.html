Sourcing services

Your purchasing desk inside Türkiye

We work on the buyer's side of the table, on a commission agreed before we start — not as a trading company reselling you goods at an undisclosed margin.

The services

Six things we do for you

Supplier search

We look for the right plant or farm for your item in its own producing region, including those that never appear online.

Supplier evaluation

We compare the shortlist on price, specification, capacity and discipline, and rule out anyone whose goods will not clear your market.

Commercial negotiation

We negotiate price, payment terms and production time in Turkish, and fix what was agreed in a written purchase order.

Quality control

Samples, laboratory analyses and a site inspection of the actual goods before loading, with a photographed report.

Export documentation

Preparing and checking the health certificates, certificate of origin, packing list and bill of lading before departure.

Logistics coordination

Booking with the carrier and following the loading, the container and its temperature through to the port of arrival.

Terms of work

How the relationship is set up

Everything in writing

A verbal agreement is worth nothing at the port.

  • Specification and grade in the purchase order
  • Packing format and net weight
  • Batch number printed and matching the analysis
  • Container temperature on the shipping order
  • Production or lay date and remaining shelf life
  • The commission, agreed before we start

What we do not do

Saying this plainly saves everyone a month.

  • We do not sell you our own stock at an undisclosed margin
  • We do not put forward a facility we have not stood inside
  • We do not quote a price without the specification behind it
  • We do not invent transit times; we take them from the carrier
  • We do not promise a certificate we have not seen
