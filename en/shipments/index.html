Shipment record

Our shipments

We document every shipment with photographs and with the item and destination data. This section is updated with each one, so it becomes a record you can look back at instead of a general promise.

We do not publish our clients' shipments here

Every shipment is documented on our side with photographs and with the item and destination data, but publishing it openly reveals what our clients buy and from where — and that is their information, not ours. Ask us for photographs of past shipments in an area close to yours and we will send them to you directly, with a real inspection report, once anything identifying the client has been removed.

What we document

What this record will hold

Every shipment has a file we keep and share a copy of with you; what is published here is whatever the client agrees to.

Shipment data

Information describing the shipment without disclosing anything about the client or their prices.

  • The sourcing area and the main items
  • Approximate quantity and number of containers
  • Port of loading and port of arrival
  • Loading date and arrival date
  • The producing region in Türkiye
  • Temperature range for chilled shipments

The photographic record

The same material the client receives before the loading instruction.

  • Photographs from inside the farm or packing line
  • The goods packed and ready before loading
  • Packing and stacking on the pallet
  • The inspection report with our notes
  • Laboratory analysis carrying the batch number
  • The container before the doors are closed
