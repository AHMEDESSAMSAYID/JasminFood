About us

A food sourcing desk, not a commission broker

We are not a trading company reselling you goods at a margin we do not disclose. We are the buyer's side of the table, working for the importer inside Türkiye on an agreed commission.

The short version

We started from a problem we know from both sides

On one side an importer in the Gulf who cannot tell a serious supplier from a trader with a website, cannot read a Turkish specification sheet, and finds out what he actually bought when the container is opened. On the other side a Turkish farm or plant that produces well, exports badly, answers slowly in a language its customer does not read, and loses the order to someone with better marketing.

What sits between them is not a catalogue. It is somebody on the ground who speaks Turkish, knows which region grows what and in which month, walks into the packing house, counts and weighs a sample from the actual consignment, and puts what was agreed in writing before anybody pays. That is the whole job.

What we are and are not

Two lines that decide everything else

What we do

The buyer's side of the table, on a commission agreed before we start.

  • Shortlist suppliers and rule out those your market will not clear
  • Negotiate in Turkish and follow up daily
  • Visit the farm or plant and photograph what we see
  • Fix the full specification in writing before production or packing
  • Inspect, count and sample the actual goods before loading
  • Arrange health certificates and batch analyses
  • Follow the shipment through to the port of arrival

What we do not do

Saying this plainly saves everyone a month.

  • We do not sell you our own stock at an undisclosed margin
  • We do not put forward a facility we have not stood inside
  • We do not quote a price without the specification behind it
  • We do not invent transit times; we take them from the carrier at booking
  • We do not publish our clients' shipments to advertise ourselves
  • We do not promise a certificate we have not seen
Why us

For every familiar problem, a defined step

Every line is something we actually do on every shipment.

The goods arrive a grade below the sample you approved.
We count, weigh and measure a sample from the actual consignment rather than from the offer sample, and photograph it before the doors close.
You buy "Malatya apricots" or "Turkish hazelnuts" and have no idea what grade.
We do not accept an offer without a written specification — grade, moisture, packing and batch number — so you compare figures, not the names of regions.
The goods arrive sound and then stop for a missing document.
We review the destination's requirements and the establishment registration before booking, not once the container is on the quay.
The reefer arrives at a temperature nobody agreed to.
We require a data logger inside the container and ask for its record at discharge, so any dispute rests on a document rather than on recollection.
The supplier stops replying, or the negotiation stalls on language.
We negotiate in Turkish and follow up daily, and go to the site ourselves when it is needed.
You cannot tell what the middleman is actually taking off the source price.
We work on a commission agreed in advance. Nothing hidden is added to the price you see.
