What we do

And what we are not

A buying office

We stand on the importer's side of the table.

  • We find the plant or farm for your item in its producing region
  • We negotiate in Turkish and follow up daily
  • We visit and photograph before recommending
  • We inspect and sample the goods before loading
  • We arrange the certificates and the batch analyses
  • We follow the shipment to the port of arrival

Why an office inside Türkiye

Food is a regional trade, not a national one.

  • Apricots come from Malatya, figs from Aydın, hazelnuts from the Black Sea
  • Apples from Isparta and Karaman, eggs from Afyonkarahisar
  • Each region has its own season and its own grading language
  • Many producers never appear online and answer only in Turkish
  • A specification is verified on site, not over email
  • The destination's requirements are checked before the booking
