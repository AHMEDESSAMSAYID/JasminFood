How we work

Six steps, from your enquiry to the port

Each step has one owner and one clear output. No stage passes without you knowing what happened in it and what comes next.

1

We take in the enquiry

The item, the grade, the quantity and the destination. The season is part of it: what is available in October is not what is available in May.

What we need from you

Your item list, approximate quantities, the grade or specification if you have one, and the final destination.

What reaches you at the end

An acknowledgement within 24 hours, with the questions we need answered before we start — the season first among them.

2

We shortlist three suppliers

We rule out anyone whose goods will not clear your market before showing them to you.

What we need from you

Nothing. This stage is entirely ours.

What reaches you at the end

Three shortlisted suppliers, why each was chosen, what distinguishes it, and what we ruled out on its account.

3

We visit and photograph

A real visit to the farm or the packing line in its season. You see what you are buying before you pay.

What we need from you

Your view on the shortlist and which supplier you want visited first.

What reaches you at the end

Photographs and video from the farm or packing line, and our notes on capacity, discipline and storage conditions.

4

We send the offer, sample and analysis

A written offer carrying the full specification — grade, moisture, packing — with a sample and a laboratory analysis.

What we need from you

Your decision on the offer, and any change to grade or packing before it is fixed.

What reaches you at the end

A written offer carrying the full specification, with a sample or a laboratory analysis depending on the item.

5

We inspect and measure before shipment

Counting, weighing and measuring on the actual goods, and a photographed report before the doors close.

What we need from you

Nothing. We inspect on your behalf.

What reaches you at the end

A photographed inspection report: counts, weights and measurements on the actual goods, firmness or moisture readings, and container photographs before the doors close.

6

We follow through to the port

Certificates, analyses, the booking and the container temperature record, followed daily to arrival.

What we need from you

Nothing beyond your customs broker's details at the port of arrival.

What reaches you at the end

The complete document set — health certificate, batch analyses, certificate of origin and bill of lading — and daily follow-up to arrival.

What runs through all of it

Two things that never change

Everything in writing

A verbal agreement is worth nothing at the port.

  • The specification and grade in the purchase order
  • The packing format and net weight
  • The batch number printed and matching the analysis
  • The container temperature on the shipping order
  • The production or lay date and the remaining shelf life
  • The commission, agreed before we start

Nothing on trust alone

We verify with our own hands, then send you what we saw.

  • A weighed and measured sample from the actual consignment
  • A laboratory analysis carrying the batch number
  • The container pre-cooled before loading starts
  • A data logger inside the container
  • Photographs before the doors close
  • The certificate seen before it is promised

Start with one item

You will see the whole process on a small order before you commit to a container.
