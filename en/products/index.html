Common to every area

What we do with any item, whatever its area

Before you pay

The stage where most of the money is lost, and the one most often skipped.

  • We verify the facility's registration and certificates ourselves
  • We visit and photograph before we recommend
  • We fix the full specification in writing, not by trade name
  • We check the destination's requirements before booking
  • We state the commission before we start

Before it ships

Inspection on the actual goods, not on the offer sample.

  • Counting, weighing and measuring a sample from the consignment
  • A laboratory analysis carrying the same batch number
  • The container pre-cooled and a data logger inside it
  • Packing and stacking checked before the doors close
  • A photographed report that reaches you before departure
